Hello! Here's a rundown on how the game works:

This is an isometric turn based strategy game where you take turns playing against the Enemy AI until either your units or the enemy's units are all dead. You win the level by killing all the enemy units before the enemy kills all your units. Losing repeats the same level and winning lets you move on to the next level.

Controls and Gameplay:

On the editor, you can use your mouse to click on tiles and your WASD keys to control the camera.

On mobile, you can use your finger to press on tiles and the joystick to control the camera.

The end turn button will end your turn and let the enemy play their turn. Use this when you are sure you are done with your turn!

Clicking on an ally unit reveals where it can move and the tiles it can possibly attack when it is your turn. Clicking on an enemy unit reveals the area that the unit can potentially attack.

After clicking on one of your units, clicking on a blue tile will move the unit to that tile. A unit cannot move to a tile that already has a unit on it.

The undo move button allows you to undo a selected unit's move if the unit has not attacked already, allowing you to change your move.

After moving, red tiles will appear around your unit which indicates the tiles that the unit can attack, if there is an enemy on it. If there is, you can click on it to attack!

If you click on a blue tile that has an enemy unit, and that tile is within the unit's attack range, the unit will attack. 

Units can counterattack if the unit that attacked it is within attack range.

You cannot move after attacking or undo a move if another unit is on the tile you are trying to undo to.

The text in the top left indicates whos turn it is.

I recommend taking the time to look around to see how many units there are around the map and their locations! The first scene to load is the Level 1 scene.
